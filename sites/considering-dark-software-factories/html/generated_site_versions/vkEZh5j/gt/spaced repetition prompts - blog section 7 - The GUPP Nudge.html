Blog Post -- Section 7 - The GUPP Nudge

What is the solution for Claude code not running an agent's Hook, and instead waiting for user input? ? GUPP Nudge

When an agent boots up, what are the two things it must read? ? Its Inbox and Hook.

What principle are workers prompted to follow regarding startup behavior? ? link not tracked — if their hook has work, they must start working without waiting.

What does gt nudge do technically? ? Sends a tmux notification to a worker (or a whole channel) as if the user had typed it.

What two mechanisms together keep GUPP running reliably? ? The GUPP Nudge and the hierarchical heartbeat from the Deacon downward.

What enables workers to continue molecules across sessions? ? GUPP — as long as work is on their hook, any new session picks it up and continues.
