Term -- Session

^ Term -- Session

Session: An ephemeral Claude Code session that Gas Town throws at persistent work. Sessions are the "cattle" in the pets vs cattle metaphor — disposable and interchangeable. The persistent identity and state belong to the agent, not the session.

  • Annotated version of 'Welcome to Gas Town'

    In Gas Town, an Agent is not a session. Term -- Sessions are ephemeral; they are the “cattle” in the Kubernetes pets vs cattle metaphor. Claude Code sessions are the cattle that Gas Town throws at persistent work. That work all lives in Beads, along with the persistent identities of the workers, and the Town Mail, the event system, and even the ephemeral orchestration, as we will see.
