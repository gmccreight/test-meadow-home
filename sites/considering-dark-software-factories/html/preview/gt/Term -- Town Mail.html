Term -- Town Mail

^ Term -- Town Mail

Bead-backed messaging between Gas Town agents via inboxes. Town mail and messaging (events) use Beads, as do other types of orchestration. As the Overseer, you have an identity in the system, and your own inbox, and you can send and receive town mail.
