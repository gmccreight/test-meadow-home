Term -- Gastown Universal Propulsion Principle - GUPP

^ Term -- Gastown Universal Propulsion Principle (GUPP)

GUPP: The mechanism that keeps all Gas Town agents moving forward through their work. Each worker checks its own state, decides its next step, and acts — then loops. It's effective but still a bit flaky, which is why The Witness exists to help smooth things out when agents get stuck.

    In Gas Town, an agent is a Bead, an identity with a singleton global address. It has some slots, including a pointer to its Role Bead (which has priming information etc. for that role), its mail inbox (all Beads), its Hook (also a Bead, used for Term -- Gastown Universal Propulsion Principle - GUPP), and some administrative stuff like orchestration state (labels and notes). The history of everything that agent has done is captured in Git, and in Beads.
