Blog Post -- Section 15 - Gas Town Workflow

What is the most fundamental workflow in Gas Town? ? The handoff — gt handoff, /handoff, or just say "let's hand off".

What happens when a worker performs a handoff? ? It optionally sends itself work, then restarts its Session right there in tmux.

Which workers require you to explicitly tell them it's time to hand off? ? The Mayor, your Crew, and sometimes others you direct.
