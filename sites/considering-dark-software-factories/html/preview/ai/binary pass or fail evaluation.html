  • b software factories and the agentic moment - 2026-02

    Because much of the software we grow itself has an agentic component, we transitioned from boolean definitions of success ("the test suite is green") to a probabilistic and empirical one. We use the term satisfaction to quantify this validation: of all the observed trajectories through all the scenarios, what fraction of them likely satisfy the user? ... not a binary pass or fail evaluation but rather a more loose definition.
