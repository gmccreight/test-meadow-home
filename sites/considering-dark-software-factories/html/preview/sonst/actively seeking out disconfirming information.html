    One of the best ways to do this is using writing to think, but in a very specific way. You don't just write a journal entry in a page every day. Rather, you attempt to think in concepts. You can write a journal, but instead of having it be a linear narrative, you either break out separate pages for distinct concepts, or if you already have pages for those concepts, you link to them. You consider those concepts. You compare and contrast them. You apply lenses like lens -- significance and implications or lens -- causes and effects. If you link to an existing concept, you open it up and look at it. You decide if what you just learned influences that concept in some meaningful way. You challenge your earlier thinking and you start actively seeking out disconfirming information to help push your understanding of those concepts forward.
