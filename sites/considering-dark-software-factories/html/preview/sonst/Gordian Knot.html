Gordian Knot

From AI: The Gordian Knot is a legendary, impossibly intricate knot tied to an ancient wagon in Gordium (circa 333 BC), which prophecy claimed would be untied only by the future ruler of Asia. Alexander the Great solved it not by untying it, but by slicing it in half with his sword, creating the phrase "cutting the Gordian knot" as a metaphor for solving a complex problem with a bold, unconventional stroke.
