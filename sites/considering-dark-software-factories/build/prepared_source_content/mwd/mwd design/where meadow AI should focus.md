[[meadow AI opportunity]]

First, there are the [[leaf site page]]s and the [[frontier]].  The question the AI should try to answer is whether the content at the frontier is [[cohesive with the site's theme]].  This will allow it to surface specific things for review in the [[app component -- site page views]].  For example, when you show the frontier pages in the graph view using [[to review - show frontier pages]], the AI could help you determine which [[source page]]s to include, and warn you against the ones that are not cohesive (and mark them as [[sensitive site page]])

Second, because of the careful [[meadow change management]] we can focus AI on helping the [[publisher]] manage change over time.  For example, after publishing a graph, new edges or pages in the [[source graph]] could lead to [[inconsistent curation state -- untracked site page within controlled zone]].  The AI could help the [[publisher]] stay on top of the changes, highlighting ones that make sense to include or exclude.

Third, AI can help with [[link modification]], and even [[block modification]].  In cases like [[link type - internal - out-link - quietly hidden]], the [[source page section -- body]] could get a little strange if the link is just totally omitted.  AI could help smooth that out by making minor adjustments to the text.  It could also maintain those minor adjustments over time as things change.
