Key prerequisites that enabled cultural evolution leading to the [[Industrial Revolution]]:

1. [[universal education]]
2. Universities and formal knowledge transmission
3. High-trust society enabling cooperation X [[con - high trust society]]
4. Reduced kinship ties allowing [[geographic mobility]]
5. Guild systems and apprenticeships
6. [[epistemological evolution]] (improved [[scientific method]]s)

The absence of these prerequisites in other advanced societies (like [[China]] or the [[Middle East]]) may explain why the Industrial Revolution began in Europe.
