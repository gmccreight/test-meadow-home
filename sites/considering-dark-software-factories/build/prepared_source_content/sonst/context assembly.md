^ [[context engineering]] -- context assembly

---

**Context assembly** is the work of gathering, shaping, and presenting the context that an agent or person needs before the main reasoning work happens.

One form is [[context assembly as research staging]], where the goal is to pull source material into an inspectable working corpus before synthesis.
