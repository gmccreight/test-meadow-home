^ multi [[site]] management

---

Part of a broader theme of [[meadow change management]]

Multi site management refers to the capability to manage multiple [[site]]s effectively. This concept is closely tied to <span class="link-not-tracked">link not tracked</span> and enables users to:

- Apply <span class="link-not-tracked">link not tracked</span>s across multiple sites
- Manage sensitive content across publications
- [[managing changes in the source graph structure over time]]
	- perhaps you'd be fine with just adding some specific, <span class="link-not-tracked">link not tracked</span> pages to all the sites and re-publishing all of them
	- or you know you deleted some page and you'd be happy to remove it from being <span class="link-not-tracked">link not tracked</span> in all the graphs.  Or maybe some visualization would help you know whether it would be ok to remove.
