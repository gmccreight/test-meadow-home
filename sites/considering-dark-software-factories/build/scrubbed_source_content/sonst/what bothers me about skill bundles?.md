The thing that bothers me about [[project that is a bundle of skills|projects that are a bundle of skills]] is that they are like software frameworks as opposed to software libraries.

At their best, they are actually just concepts that are executable. I suspect that most won't fit, unmodified, into an *existing* opinionated workflow. If you have no opinions, then I suppose it's pretty easy to adopt them whole hog.

The shame of it is that there are many good nuggets in these skill bundles, and those nuggets could be [[Recontextualization|recontextualized]], but the authors haven't written them that way. Instead of pushing concepts, they are pushing these big bundles of skills.  They write them like they are the one true way.  It's like poorly-factored code, where do you have to install the whole library even if you want a single function.
