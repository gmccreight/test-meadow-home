^ [[coding concept]] -- the happy zone

---

[[person - J B Rainsberger]]'s name for the internal ring in [[hexagonal-architecture]] or [[clean-architecture]]. Consists only of state-based tests or communication and contract tests (isolated).

Seems similar to what [[person - Avdi Grimm]] talks about in [[book-confident-ruby]].  When you "protect the borders", this is what's in the inside.

The happy zone is great, because it isn't littered with [[guard clause]]s and assertions to protect against numerous edge cases.  It is very straightforward, and easy to read.
