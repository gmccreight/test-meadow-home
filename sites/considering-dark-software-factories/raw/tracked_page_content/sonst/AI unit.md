### Why not "model"?

I like avoiding [[model]] because these days (early 2026) most of these units we interact with are [[compound system]]s like [[AI unit -- GPT-5]].

### Why not "agent"?

I'm also reticent to call them [[agent]]s because I believe agents are perhaps a sub-categorization of these AI units.  They are, specifically, a more complex [[compound system]] of more basic types of AI units.  For example, people would call something like [[project - Claude Code]] an agent because it is an  [[agent harness]].  So, I'd like to reserve agent for more complex compound systems.

If we consider [[AI unit as car engine metaphor]], agents feel like more than just the engine.
