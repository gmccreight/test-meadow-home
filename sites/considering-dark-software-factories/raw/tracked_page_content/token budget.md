**token budget** is the amount of model input, output, and inference spend available to a workflow, agent, or user.

A larger budget can buy more context, more retries, more verification, more parallel attempts, or more exploratory work. This is why a [[dim - token budget -- high]] can sometimes let someone [[living in the future|live in the future]] before the same workflow is economically normal.

See also [[dim - token budget]].
